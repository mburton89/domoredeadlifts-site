My Deadlift Story

How building strength healed my back

A lifetime of back pain

Growing up, I dealt with back pain and difficulty sleeping due to C-shaped scoliosis. Finding a comfortable sleeping position was often a challenge.

Summer 2023: A back injury

In the summer of 2023, I dove into a river and hyperextended my back, bending backward so far that my heels went behind my head.

The injury left me with persistent back pain for six months or longer. The pain was especially uncomfortable at night, making it difficult to find a comfortable sleeping position. I also held back from exercising at my full potential.

I stopped doing lower-back and core exercises because they hurt, believing my back needed rest. Unfortunately, I wasn't seeing any improvement.

December 2023: Discovering deadlifts

In December 2023, I spoke with a former MMA fighter who shared his own experience with back pain. He told me that a back surgeon had recommended trying deadlifts with proper form for several months before considering surgery.

According to his experience, deadlifting had made his back pain disappear.

That conversation inspired me to give deadlifts a try myself, starting carefully and focusing on proper technique.

One month later: A turning point

Within about a month of starting deadlifts, my back pain had completely disappeared.

But the benefits didn't stop there. I began developing a stronger physique, building muscle, and improving my overall strength.

For me, deadlifts became a turning point in how I thought about my back. Rather than avoiding movement out of fear, I began focusing on building strength.

Building a balanced body

Deadlifts were the biggest factor in my experience, but I also came to appreciate the importance of balanced strength training.

Training your posterior chain, including your back and hamstrings, is only part of the picture. Your body benefits from a well-rounded approach that also includes your chest, triceps, quadriceps, and abdominal muscles.

I've continued strengthening my core and building a more balanced physique, and my back feels better than it has in years.

Why I created Burtonian Deadlifts

I created Burtonian Deadlifts to share my progress, demonstrate proper deadlift technique, and help others develop strength with confidence.

I want to make learning the deadlift approachable and help people track their progress as they build stronger bodies.

My experience showed me how much strength training could change my life. Now, I want to share what I've learned and encourage others to explore what building strength can do for them.
